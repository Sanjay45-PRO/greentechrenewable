PM Surya Ghar Yojana

Solar panels with government subsidy support. Every home, zero bill.

₹1,38,000

maximum subsidy for eligible households. Free electricity up to 300 units a month, and savings of up to ₹15,000 a year.

How we get you there

  1. Check eligibility. Send us your bill and roof details on WhatsApp.
  2. Site survey. We size the system and give a clear quote.
  3. Installation. Our team installs and commissions it.
  4. Net metering and DISCOM approval. We handle the documents and follow-ups.
  5. Subsidy. We support your claim under the scheme.
