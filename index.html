Questions, answered

What types of solar systems do you install?

Rooftop grid-connected plants, off-grid systems, solar water pumping and irrigation, street and home lighting, water heaters and solar air conditioners, for homes, farms and industry.

Is there a government subsidy?

Yes. Eligible households can claim a subsidy under the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana. We check your eligibility and handle the application. Exact amounts depend on system size and current scheme rules.

Do you help with net metering and DISCOM approval?

Yes. We prepare the documents, coordinate with the DISCOM and support you until the net meter is installed.

How long does installation take?

A typical home rooftop system is installed within days of site survey and approvals. Larger plants take longer. We give you a written schedule up front.

How does solar reduce carbon emissions?

Every unit of solar electricity replaces a unit that would otherwise come from the grid, which in India is still largely coal-based. Fewer units from fossil fuel means lower emissions.

What maintenance does a system need?

Very little: periodic panel cleaning and a routine inspection of inverter and wiring. We offer maintenance support after commissioning.

Ready to switch to solar?

Tell us your monthly bill. We will size the system, handle approvals and the subsidy.
