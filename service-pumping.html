Overview

A solar pumping system turns sunlight into electricity that runs a motor pump set directly. It draws water from open wells, bore wells, ponds and canals, so irrigation and drinking water no longer depend on grid supply or fuel.

  • No electricity bill and no diesel cost for pumping
  • Runs automatically from sunrise to sunset
  • Works with open wells, bore wells, ponds and canals
  • Low maintenance with few moving parts
  • Suited to remote farms where grid power is weak or missing

Main components

Solar arrayConverts sunlight into DC power. Sized to the pump rating and mounted on a galvanised structure.
Pump controllerAdjusts motor speed to the available sunlight and protects against dry run, overload and over-voltage.
Motor-pump setLifts water from the source. Submersible sets suit deep bore wells; surface sets suit open wells and ponds.
Water storageA tank or sump so water is available when sunlight is low.
Level sensor or floatCuts the pump automatically when the tank is full.
Cables, earthing and protectionUV-resistant cables, proper earthing and lightning protection for safe, long-term running.
