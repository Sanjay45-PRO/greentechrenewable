Overview

Air conditioning is the biggest load on most electricity bills. A solar air conditioner uses panels to supply a large share of the power it needs, so your cooling costs fall sharply in the hours when the sun is strongest.

  • Targets your largest electricity load
  • Cooling peaks when sunshine peaks
  • Hybrid option keeps cooling on at night and in cloud
  • Lower bills and lower emissions
  • Suited to homes, shops, clinics and offices

How solar air conditioning works

A simple path from sunlight to indoor comfort.

System componentsSolar PV panelsSolar or hybrid inverterCompatible air conditionerOptional battery storage

Main components

Solar panelsSupply DC power. Array size is matched to the air conditioner’s running load.
Hybrid controller or inverterChooses between solar, grid and battery and feeds the compressor.
Inverter air conditionerIndoor and outdoor units with a variable-speed compressor.
Battery (optional)Extends cooling after sunset or during a power cut.
Grid connectionKeeps the room cool whenever solar output is low.
