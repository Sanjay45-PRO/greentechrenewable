Overview

A rooftop system generates power where you use it. Grid-connected plants feed surplus back through net metering, and off-grid or hybrid versions add batteries for backup. We handle design, installation, approvals and subsidy paperwork.

  • Bill reduction of up to 90% for a well-sized system
  • Eligible homes can claim PM Surya Ghar subsidy
  • Net metering and DISCOM approval handled by us
  • Panels last 25 years or more with little upkeep
  • Fits homes, shops, factories, schools and hospitals
