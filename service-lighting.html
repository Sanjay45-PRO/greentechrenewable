Overview

Solar lights charge from the sun during the day and switch on automatically at night. They suit streets, villages, gardens, campuses and homes, and can be installed where laying cable is costly or impossible.

  • No cabling or trenching
  • Automatic dusk-to-dawn operation
  • Zero running cost
  • Keeps working during power cuts
  • Suited to roads, parks, farms and compounds

How a solar light works

It charges in the day and lights up at night, all on its own.

Daytime charging

SunlightFalls on the panel
Solar panelCollects the energy
BatteryStores it for the night

Dusk switch-on

DuskLight level drops
ControllerSensor and timer react
LED lightSwitches on

Night lighting

BatteryPowers the LED
LED lightBright, even light
Road or pathSafe and visible

Solar panelSized to recharge the battery fully on an average day at your location.

BatteryLithium or tubular battery with several nights of backup.
