Overview

A solar water heater collects heat from the sun and stores hot water in an insulated tank. It replaces electric geysers and gas heaters in homes, hostels, hotels and hospitals, and has very low running cost.

  • Big cut in water heating cost
  • Hot water stored for use after sunset
  • Long life with simple upkeep
  • Electric backup option for cloudy spells
  • Suited to homes, hostels, hotels and hospitals

How a solar water heater works

Hot water rises on its own. No pump is needed in a standard home system.
